## Brand & Style

The brand identity honors Karakalpak cultural lineage filtered through an ultra-contemporary, Michelin-caliber hospitality lens. Designed for discerning patrons, epicures, and diaspora seeking high-end Uzbek and Karakalpak culinary heritage, the aesthetic bridges nomadic authenticity and refined luxury dining.

The interface combines **Minimalist High-Contrast** architecture with **Tactile Glass & Glow** nuances:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, moody, and intimate—reminiscent of velvet twilight over the Amu Darya and amber-lit banquet tables.
- **Visual Weight:** Expansive negative space allows cinematic, high-saturation food photography (such as Beshbarmak, Aksawlaq, and spiced lamb ribs) to anchor the experience.
- **Cultural Texture:** Micro-ornamentation drawn from traditional Karakalpak geometric motifs (shiyberek patterns) executed via fine hairline gold vectors rather than folkloric clutter.

## Colors

The palette employs a rich, dark-dominant scheme rooted in organic earth, ember tones, and heirloom brass.

- **Foundational Surfaces:** 
  - Canvas Root: `#17120E` (Deep obsidian charcoal-brown)
  - Card & Container Tiers: `#1F1914` (Surface elevated) and `#261F19` (Surface interactive/hover)
  - Modal & Sheet Overlays: `#2E261F` with 80% opacity and contextual blur
- **Precious Metals & Accents:**
  - Primary Brass: `#D4AF37` reserved for primary calls-to-action, active navigational states, and signature pricing badges.
  - Radiant Amber: `#E5B842` for interactive focus rings, illuminated glowing indicators, and special tasting menu highlights.
  - Burnished Gold: `#C59B27` for borders, hairline dividers, and muted secondary iconography.
- **Editorial Contrast:**
  - High-tier text and headers use Cream Silk (`#FAF7F2`).
  - Secondary labels and descriptors use Warm Linen (`#F3EDE2` at 70% opacity).
  - Diminished metadata utilizes Muted Ochre (`#A89F91`).

## Typography

The type system creates friction-free scanning for ordering alongside editorial grandeur:
- **Headlines:** Set in `Playfair Display`, calibrated for dramatic, welcoming presentation of course names, regional tasting menus, and culinary storytelling. Italic accents are encouraged for provenance designations (e.g., *Mo'ynaq balig'i*, *Qaraqalpaq palawı*).
- **Interface & Operational Data:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Delivers legibility for micro-modifiers, dietary tags, caloric values, and financial transactions in Uzbek So'm (`UZS`).
- **Formatting Guidance:** Dish titles use Sentence Case in headline serif; currency amounts and culinary tags (`YANGI`, `CHEF TAVSIYASI`, `MAVSUMIY`) are set in uppercase Sans with increased letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a responsive 12-column grid on desktop screens, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fluid container bounded by an inner max-width of `1440px`. Dish categories and basket summaries operate within sticky columns flanking a central gastronomic showcase.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid; bottom sticky dock holds cart metrics and order initiation.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with single-column linear order flow. Card elements utilize edge-to-edge full width with internal paddings of `space-md`.
- **Rhythm & Proximity:** High vertical relief between dish sections (`space-xl`) prevents visual fatigue and honors the relaxed pacing of upscale dining.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient warmth and illuminated materials instead of generic grey drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid charcoal `#17120E`, raw, absorbant, dark.
- **Level 1 (Dish & Category Cards):** `#1F1914` elevated with a 1px border rendered in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Flyout Drawer):** `#261F19` backdrop blur (`16px`), accompanied by a soft, diffused ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -8px rgba(10, 7, 5, 0.7), 0 0 20px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Customization Sheets & Checkout Flow):** Deep obsidian glass `#2E261F` with dual edge highlighting: a crisp 1px perimeter border `rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.25)` and a directional top-edge bevel of `rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces generous, organic curves that echo bespoke handcrafted tableware and clay tandoor silhouettes:

- Standard input fields, quantity counter pills, and interactive chips use sweeping pill radii (`9999px` or level `3`).
- Cards, modal containers, and floating imagery employ `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to `rounded-3xl` (2rem / 32px), creating a velvety visual finish against the dark background.
- Dish photography containers adopt asymmetrical rounding in featured showcases (e.g., `rounded-tl-3xl rounded-br-3xl`) for editorial rhythm.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Buyurtma berish" / Checkout):** Full gold pill (`#D4AF37`), dark charcoal text (`#17120E`), font weight 600. On hover, transitions to vibrant amber (`#E5B842`) with an outer halo: `0 0 16px rgba(229, 184, 66, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary ("Savatga qo'shish" / Add to Cart):** Deep container `#261F19` with a 1px burnished border (`#C59B27`) and warm cream text (`#FAF7F2`).
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Transparent ground, underline on hover, gold typography with subtle arrow transitions.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Navigation filters (*"Milliy Taomlar"*, *"Kabablar"*, *"Desertlar"*, *"Ichimliklar"*) use pill containers. Unselected: `#1F1914` with faint amber border. Selected: Solid `#D4AF37` with `#17120E` typography and gentle scale bounce.

### Dish Presentation Cards
- Dark surface background (`#1F1914`) framed in 1px `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`.
- Upper quadrant: 16:10 or 1:1 ratio ratio ultra-high-definition food imagery with a slight bottom vignette gradient bleeding into the card body.
- Content zone: Serif title in `#FAF7F2`, short poetic description in `#A89F91`, and the price badge placed in crisp sans-serif bold typography highlighted in `#E5B842`.

### Inputs & Counter Controls
- Numeric dish steppers (- / +) are unified pill structures in `#261F19` with brass iconography.
- Text fields (Special instructions / *"Oshpazga eslatma"*): Charcoal field `#17120E` inset with 1px border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`; activates to solid gold border `#D4AF37` upon focus with zero layout shift.

### Badges & Micro-Tags
- Authentic Uzbek tags (e.g., *"Halol"*, *"Mualliflik taomi"*) framed in micro-capsules: gold border, translucent amber fill `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`, and uppercase 10px tracking.
